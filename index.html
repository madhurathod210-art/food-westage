Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Is there any fee to collect or donate food?

Never. The Food Bridge network is 100% free, public, and powered by mutual community aid.

❓ How fast can volunteers pick up surplus catering?

Our average rescue dispatch response time is under 35 minutes for metro area donors.

❓ Are businesses protected from liability?

Yes. Under Good Samaritan legislation, donors acting in good faith are fully protected from liability.

❓ How do I host a community fridge at my business?

Contact our coordinator desk using this form. We provide the refrigerator, signage, and maintenance support!
